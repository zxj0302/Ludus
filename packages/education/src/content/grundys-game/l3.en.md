# Grundy's Game — Full Mathematics and Algorithms

## 1. Play

**Grundy's Game** (normal play): a finite multiset of heap sizes $\{n_1,\ldots,n_k\}$, $n_i\ge 1$.

Legal move: pick $n_i\ge 3$, replace it by $a,b$ with $a+b=n_i$, $a,b\ge 1$, $a\neq b$ (implementation uses $1\le a\le\lfloor(n_i-1)/2\rfloor$).

Terminal: every $n_i\le 2$ (no moves) → the player to move loses.

Hub uses `phase: 'choosing'` for hand choice. Victory is **Nim-isomorphic**: after the last move, `currentPlayer` is the side that cannot move; human just won iff `currentPlayer===1`.

## 2. Think

### 2.1 Questions

- How is $g(n)$ defined rigorously, and why is the sum XOR?
- Does the sequence of $g(n)$ eventually become periodic? (Open; out of scope.)
- How should the generator bias P vs N?

### 2.2 Intuition

A split replaces one component by the **disjunctive sum** of two; Sprague–Grundy says its value is $g(a)\oplus g(b)$. Taking mex over all legal splits yields $g(n)$.

## 3. Reveal

### 3.1 Formalism

**Single heap:**

$$g(n)=\operatorname{mex}\{g(a)\oplus g(b)\mid a+b=n,\,a>0,\,b>0,\,a\neq b\}$$

**Position theorem:** multi-heap is P $\iff$ $g(n_1)\oplus\cdots\oplus g(n_k)=0$.

### 3.2 Versus Nim / Euclid / Wythoff

| | Nim | Euclid | Wythoff | Grundy (this module) |
|--|-----|--------|---------|----------------------|
| Move | remove from one heap | subtract $k\times$ smaller | one or equal both | split into unequal pair |
| Test | XOR of sizes | φ band | Beatty | **XOR of nimbers** |
| Lesson | Bouton | φ / gcd | φ / Beatty | **SG theorem itself** |

### 3.3 Implementation

- Memoized `computeGrundy(n)`; Hub levels keep $n\le 24$.
- Unit tests match OEIS [A002188](https://oeis.org/A002188) for $n=0..20$.
- Periodicity conjectures (*Winning Ways*, Flammenkamp) are **not** proved here.
