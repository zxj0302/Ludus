# Kayles — L3

## 1–2. Play & Think

## 3. Formalization

State: `alive: boolean[]`. Moves knock index $i$ (one pin) or $i,i+1$ (two standing adjacent pins).

Contiguous `true` runs have lengths $\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_k$. By Sprague–Grundy:

$$\text{position} \equiv *\bigl(g(\ell_1)\oplus\cdots\oplus g(\ell_k)\bigr).$$

Single-chain recurrence (Wikipedia / OEIS A002186):

$$
g(n)=\operatorname{mex}\Biggl(
  \{g(i)\oplus g(n-1-i):0\le i<n\}
  \cup
  \{g(i)\oplus g(n-2-i):0\le i\le n-2\}
\Biggr),\quad g(0)=0.
$$

Kayles is octal **`.77`**. **Dawson's Kayles** (knock exactly two) is **out of scope**.

| Game | Move | OEIS |
|------|------|------|
| Nim | reduce a heap | — |
| Grundy's Game | unequal split | A002188 |
| **Kayles** | knock 1 or 2 adjacent | **A002186** |

Hub caps $n\le 12$; eventual period-12 behavior for large $n$ is not required here.
